1. The Four Layers of the Box Model

Every HTML element is represented as a rectangular box. The box model consists of content, padding, border, and margin. Content contains text or images, padding creates space around the content, the border surrounds the padding, and margin creates space outside the border.

Understanding these four layers helps developers control the spacing and appearance of elements. Try adding padding, borders, and margins to this paragraph to observe how its size and position change.

3. Content-Box vs Border-Box

The box-sizing property determines how an element's width and height are calculated. With content-box, padding and borders are added to the declared width. With border-box, padding and borders are included within the declared width.
